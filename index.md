# Orbit – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 27 September 2026_

## Summary
Orbit does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere.

## Information used on your device
Orbit reads basic device status, such as battery and network connection state, only to display it on your screen. This information is processed on your phone, is never sent anywhere, and is not used for any other purpose.

## Permissions
Some permissions are needed for the app to work. The optional Phone permission is used only to read signal and network status. Orbit never reads your phone number, calls, messages, contacts or SIM identity.

## Data collection and sharing
- No personal data is collected.
- No analytics, advertising or tracking.
- No data is shared with third parties.
- Your settings stay on your phone and are removed when you uninstall the app.

## Children
Orbit is not directed at children and does not collect data from anyone.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
